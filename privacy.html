Privacy Policy

Protein Saathi · Effective 26 October 2026

In short: Protein Saathi does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own phone.

What you enter

To estimate your protein target and keep a daily record, the app asks for your sex, age, weight, height, activity level, goal, food preference, and optional health factors (pregnancy or breastfeeding, kidney disease, balance problems). You may also record foods you eat each day.

Where it is stored

All of this information is saved only in the app's local storage on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools.

Internet use

The app works offline. It may load its own program files from its website when first installed or updated. It does not send any of your entered information over the internet. Links to scientific sources open in your browser only if you tap them.

Backups

If you choose "Save a backup copy", the app creates a file on your device that you can keep or share yourself. We never receive this file.

Deleting your data

You can delete all your data at any time from Profile → Delete all my data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may also remove it.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Health disclaimer

The calculations are only indicative estimates for awareness and are not medical or dietary advice. Please take any decision about your diet, supplements or exercise in consultation with an authorised nutritionist and your doctor.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
